Show Me - Tell Me Questions

Vehicle safety questions asked by the examiner during the practical exam. Divided into two parts: Show Me (practical demonstration) and Tell Me (verbal explanation).

Show Me

Show Me

Show me how you check your tires and whether the tread depth is sufficient and the tires are in good condition.

Answer: Visually inspect all four tires, ensure the tread depth is at least 1.6 mm in the center and 1 mm on the edges, and confirm there are no cuts or bulges.

Show Me

Show me how you check that the handbrake is working properly.

Answer: Pull the handbrake and try to move the car slightly; if it does not move, the handbrake is working.

Show Me

Show me how you check that the front and rear lights are working, including the brake lights.

Answer: Turn on the low beams, main beams, brake lights, reverse lights, front and rear position lights. You can use a reflection in a window or wall, or ask someone to check.

Show Me

Show me where you check the engine oil level.

Answer: Open the bonnet, pull out the dipstick, wipe it clean, reinsert it, then pull it out and check the level — it should be between the MIN and MAX marks.

Show Me

Show me where you check the engine coolant level.

Answer: Open the bonnet and locate the coolant reservoir. Confirm the level is between MIN and MAX. Never open the cap when the engine is hot.

Show Me

Show me where you check the brake fluid level.

Answer: Open the bonnet and locate the brake fluid reservoir (usually near the back of the engine on the driver's side). Confirm the level is between MIN and MAX.

Show Me

Show me where you check the windshield washer fluid level.

Answer: Open the bonnet and locate the washer fluid reservoir (usually a blue cap or a windshield icon). Confirm there is enough fluid.

Show Me

Show me how to operate the windshield wipers and washer.

Answer: Turn on the wipers at different speeds, then activate the washer and confirm the water sprays correctly onto the windshield.

Show Me

Show me how to operate the hazard warning lights.

Answer: Press the red triangle button on the dashboard to turn on the four flashing lights on both sides.

Show Me

Show me how to operate the horn.

Answer: Press the center of the steering wheel to sound the horn, and confirm the sound is clear and audible.

Tell Me

Tell Me

Tell me how you would check that the brakes are working before starting a journey.

Answer: Press the brake pedal — it should feel firm, not spongy. When moving slowly, the car should stop smoothly when you press the brake lightly.

Tell Me

Tell me how you check your tires and when they should be replaced.

Answer: Visually inspect for cuts or bulges, ensure tread depth is at least 1.6 mm, and check the correct pressure. Replace the tire if it is damaged or older than 6 years.

Tell Me

Tell me which lights on the car must always work.

Answer: Low beams, main beams, front and rear position lights, brake lights, reverse lights, and turn signals (front and rear).

Tell Me

Tell me the recommended tire pressure and how you find it.

Answer: The recommended pressure is usually on a sticker on the driver's door frame or in the owner's manual. It typically ranges between 30 and 35 PSI.

Tell Me

Tell me how you check that the oil level is correct.

Answer: Park on level ground, turn off the engine and wait a minute, then pull the dipstick, wipe it, reinsert it, and pull it out — the level should be between MIN and MAX.

Tell Me

Tell me what you would do if the engine warning light came on while driving.

Answer: Stop in a safe place, turn off the engine, and check oil, coolant, and lights. Do not restart the engine if the light stays on, and call a mechanic.

Tell Me

Tell me what you would do if a tire burst while driving.

Answer: Hold the steering wheel firmly, ease off the accelerator, do not brake hard, steer the car safely to the side, and stop in a safe place.

Tell Me

Tell me what you would do if the brakes failed in the car.

Answer: Pump the brake pedal repeatedly, shift to a lower gear, gradually apply the handbrake, and steer the car to a safe or open area.

Tell Me

Tell me why seat belts are important.

Answer: Seat belts reduce the risk of serious injuries in a crash and prevent passengers from being thrown forward during sudden braking.

Tell Me

Tell me what the warning lights on the dashboard mean.

Answer: Red lights mean immediate danger — stop. Yellow means a warning that needs attention soon. Green means normal operation. Blue indicates information or a comfort feature.
