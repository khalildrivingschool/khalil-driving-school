Documents Required to Obtain a Driving License

  • A copy of the ID card, a copy of the individual civil registry record, or a passport.
  • Criminal record certificate (judicial record).
  • A medical certificate specific to the driving license.
  • A residency certificate from the local mukhtar.
  • 3 personal photographs certified by the mukhtar.
  • Blood type card.
  • A driving education and training certificate issued by the driving school (with the number of education and training hours).
