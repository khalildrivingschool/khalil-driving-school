Practical Exam Maneuvers

Layout of the practical exam course with an explanation of each stage — from the starting line to the finish line, including stopping, reversing, and parking.

1

Starting Line

The starting point of the course. The candidate starts from here after receiving the signal from the examiner.

2

Reverse (Point R)

The candidate must reverse a defined distance while maintaining full control of the vehicle.

3

Parking

Parking between vehicles or in a designated spot without touching the boundaries, while demonstrating precise steering and brake control.

4

Turning

Performing a sharp turn within the course limits without crossing the lines.

5

Passage

Passing through the barriers without crossing the lines or the barriers.

6

Reverse (Point R)

The candidate must reverse a defined distance while maintaining full control of the vehicle.

STOP

Parking between vehicles or in a designated spot without touching the boundaries, while demonstrating precise steering and brake control, in addition to turning off the engine.
