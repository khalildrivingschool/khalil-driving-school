Car Dashboard Warning Lights Guide

Understanding your car's dashboard warning lights is crucial for safe driving. This guide covers all the common warning lights you might encounter and what they mean.

Color Guide

Critical — Stop Immediately
Warning — Check Soon
Normal Operation
Information
Engine & Performance

Engine Temperature Warning Light

Indicator light means the engine temperature has exceeded normal limits. Check coolant level, fan operation, radiator cap, coolant leaks.

Action Stop the vehicle immediately and check coolant level
Engine & Performance

Battery Charge Warning Light

Indicator light means that the car's charging system is short of power or is not charging properly. It normally indicates a problem with the battery itself or the alternator.

Action Check battery connections and alternator
Engine & Performance

Oil Pressure Warning Light

Indicator light means loss of oil pressure, meaning lubrication is low or lost completely. Immediately check the oil level and pressure.

Action Stop immediately and check oil level
Safety Systems

Brake Warning Light

Indicator light turns on when the handbrake is on. If it lit continuously, it means that hydraulic pressure has been lost in one side of the brake system or that the fluid level in the master cylinder is dangerously low (due to a leak somewhere in the brake system).

Action Check brake fluid level and brake system
Engine & Performance

Transmission Temperature

Indicator light means the transmission temperature has exceeded normal limits. Check transmission fluid level and cooling system.

Action Stop and let transmission cool down
Safety Systems

Tire Pressure Warning Light

Indicator light means the pressure is low in one of your tires.

Action Check and inflate tires to proper pressure
Advanced Features

Traction Control Off

Indicator light means that the vehicles TCS (traction control system) has been deactivated.

Action Reactivate traction control system
Safety Systems

Steering Wheel Lock

Indicator light means your steering wheel is locked and can not be moved. To turn off the steering lock, insert the key into the ignition and turn it to at least the first position while turning the steering wheel in either direction.

Action Insert key and turn steering wheel
Vehicle Status

Trailer Tow Hitch Warning

Indicator lights mean that the tow hitch is unlocked or that there's an issue with the lighting system.

Action Check tow hitch connection and trailer lights
Advanced Features

Traction Control Light

Indicator light turns on when the vehicle TCS (traction control system) is activated.

Action Normal operation - no action needed
Vehicle Status

Service Vehicle Soon

Indicator light turns on when there is a fault condition in an area of the vehicle chassis systems such as—the anti-lock (ABS) brake system, the traction control (TCS) system, the electronic suspension system, or the brake hydraulic system.

Action Schedule service appointment
Safety Systems

Security Alert

Indicator light will turn on momentarily if the ignition switch is locked and will need the proper transponder-equipped key to restart. If the light is visible when the vehicle is on, then it typically indicates a malfunction in the security system.

Action Use proper key or contact dealer
Safety Systems

Side Airbag

Indicator light means a fault with the side airbag.

Action Contact dealer immediately
Engine & Performance

Reduced Power Warning

Indicator light means that the Engine Computer has limited engine power output.

Action Drive carefully and schedule service
Safety Systems

Seat Belt Indicator

Indicator light means that a seat belt has not been secured for a passenger in the vehicle.

Action Fasten all seat belts
Comfort & Convenience

Press Clutch Pedal

Indicator to push the clutch.

Action Press clutch pedal to start
Engine & Performance

Powertrain Fault

Indicator light turns on when a powertrain or AWD fault has been detected. Contact your mechanic as soon as possible.

Action Contact mechanic immediately
Safety Systems

Power Steering Warning Light

Indicator light turns on if there is an issue with the power steering system, and will be disabled until fixed.

Action Contact dealer for repair
Comfort & Convenience

Press Brake Pedal

Indicator to push the brake pedal.

Action Press brake pedal to start

Parking Brake Light

Indicator light means park brake is on.

Action Release parking brake before driving
Comfort & Convenience

Overdrive Light

Indicator light means that the vehicle's overdrive system has been manually turned off.

Action Normal operation - can be reactivated
Engine & Performance

Oil Change Reminder

Indicator light means that oil life has expired.

Action Schedule oil change service
Vehicle Status

Master Warning Light

Indicator light usually accompanied by another warning light and indicates that one or more warning systems have been detected.

Action Check all other warning lights
Vehicle Status

Information Warning Light

Indicator light will turn on when a new message is stored in the information display. It will be red or amber in color depending on the severity of the message and will remain on until the cause of the message has been rectified.

Action Check information display for details
Safety Systems

Icy Road Warning Light

Indicator light turns on when the outside air starts to reach freezing temperatures, around 35°F or 3°C.

Action Drive carefully on icy roads
Engine & Performance

Gas/Fuel Cap

Indicator light turns on if the gas/fuel cap is not properly tightened.

Action Tighten fuel cap properly
Advanced Features

ESP Fault / Traction Control Malfunction

Indicator light means that there is a problem with the vehicle's traction control.

Action Contact dealer for repair
Safety Systems

Electric Park Brake

Indicator light turns on when the electric parking brake has a malfunction.

Action Contact dealer for repair
Safety Systems

Distance Warning

Indicator light means that a vehicle in front is too close or is being approached too quickly, or that a stationary obstacle exists in the direction of travel.

Action Increase following distance
Engine & Performance

Clogged Air Filter

Indicator light turns on when there is reduced airflow to the engine. Have the air filter replaced or inspected.

Action Replace or clean air filter
Comfort & Convenience

Child Safety Lock

Indicator light means child safety lock is active.

Action Normal operation - can be deactivated
Engine & Performance

Check Engine or Malfunction Indicator Light (MIL)

Indicator light turns on whenever the engine is turned on to check the bulb. If the light stays illuminated, the car's diagnostic systems have detected a malfunction that needs to be investigated.

Action Have engine diagnosed by mechanic

Catalytic Converter Warning

Action Contact dealer immediately

Brake Fluid

Action Check brake fluid level and add if needed

Brake Pad Warning

Indicator light means that the brake pads are worn.

Action Replace brake pads

Automatic Gearbox Warning

Indicator light means there's a gearbox/transmission malfunction.

Action Contact dealer for transmission repair
Safety Systems

Anti-lock Braking System (ABS)

Indicator light means there may be a malfunction in the ABS system.

Action Contact dealer for ABS repair
Advanced Features

All Wheel Drive (AWD/4WD)

Action Contact dealer for 4WD system repair
Safety Systems

Airbag Indicator

Action Contact dealer for airbag system repair
Safety Systems

Airbag Deactivated

Adaptive Suspension Dampers

Indicator light means there is a need to contact an authorised repairer.

Advanced Features

4 Wheel Drive (4WD) LOCK Indicator Light

Air Suspension

Indicator light means water in fuel filter has reached maximum capacity. Drain the water from filter.

Action Drain water from fuel filter

Important Tips

  • Red lights require immediate attention - stop driving if safe to do so
  • Yellow lights indicate a problem that needs attention soon
  • Green lights show normal operation - no action needed
  • Blue lights provide information or comfort features
  • Always refer to your vehicle's manual for specific instructions
