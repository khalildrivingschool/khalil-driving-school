Theoretical Exam (Written)

A computer-based test consisting of 30 questions. The candidate must answer at least 24 questions correctly in order to pass.

  • 30 questions
  • Computer-based test
  • Passing score: 24/30

Practical Exam (Driving)

The exam is conducted by a government inspector at an official driving center. It assesses the candidate's ability to control the vehicle, awareness of the surroundings, parking skills, lane discipline, and compliance with traffic signs and laws. The test can be taken on a vehicle with manual or automatic transmission, and the type of transmission must be indicated on the license. The candidate must achieve at least 10/20 in the practical exam to pass.

  • Conducted by a government inspector
  • At an official driving center
  • Passing score: 10/20
  • Vehicle with manual or automatic transmission
